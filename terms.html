No hacking, no unauthorised access

This section is deliberately explicit, because "don't break the site" is not specific enough to be fair to anyone.

You must not attempt to access, take control of, disrupt or extract data from any Zyrex system — website, API, download infrastructure, storage buckets, Discord bots, internal admin panels or the servers behind them — other than through the interfaces we publish for ordinary use. Authorisation is required, and browsing the public site is the only authorisation you have.

Prohibited activity includes, without limitation:

  • Unauthorised entry. Logging in with credentials that aren't yours, using someone else's session or token, or reaching administrative areas, internal endpoints, debug routes or storage paths you weren't given access to.
  • Exploitation. Probing, exploiting or weaponising any vulnerability — SQL injection, SSRF, IDOR, path traversal, deserialisation, template injection, race conditions or anything comparable — instead of reporting it.
  • Authentication attacks. Credential stuffing, password spraying, brute-forcing tokens or invite codes, session hijacking, cookie forgery or bypassing Discord OAuth and pass checks.

  • Data exfiltration. Downloading, copying or scraping databases, user records, private media or non-public files you don't own.

Consequences. A breach of this section results in immediate and permanent loss of access, removal of any associated uploads, and — where the conduct is criminal — a report to the relevant authorities together with the technical records we hold. We also reserve the right to pursue civil claims for damage, recovery costs and service disruption.

Found a real vulnerability? That's useful, not hostile. Email contact@zyrexediting.xyz with the details and steps to reproduce, and give us a reasonable window to fix it before publishing anything. Good-faith reports made this way won't be treated as a breach of these Terms, and we'll credit you if you want the credit.
