Privacy Policy

You should be able to read this in one sitting and know exactly where you stand. Here is what Zyrex stores, why it exists, how long it stays, and how to have it erased.

01

Scope and who is responsible

This policy covers everything on zyrexediting.xyz, including the leaks catalogue, the plugins index, the audio library, the request board, upload tools and the Zyrex Discord server. It explains how information is handled when you browse, sign in, download or publish.

The Zyrex team is the controller of that information. Questions, requests and complaints about privacy all go to the same place: contact@zyrexediting.xyz.

02

03

What we deliberately don't collect

Just as important as the list above is the list of things that never touch our systems:

No passwords

Authentication happens on Discord's servers. We only ever receive a token and your public profile.

No payment details

Zyrex doesn't process card data. There is nothing to charge and nothing to store.

No precise location

No GPS, no device address book, no contacts, no microphone or camera access.

No advertising profiles

We don't share identifiers with data brokers or build cross-site behavioural segments.

04

Cookies and local storage

Zyrex uses three kinds of browser storage, all of them functional:

  • Session cookie — signed and HTTP-only, so scripts on the page can't read it. It holds your login state and disappears on logout or expiry.
  • Local preferences — grid or list view, liked items, theme and consent choices. Stored locally in your browser, never transmitted for profiling.
  • Consent memory — a flag so the cookie notice doesn't reappear on every page.

There are no cross-site tracking cookies, no fingerprinting scripts and no retargeting pixels on this site. Clearing site data in your browser removes everything on the local side; it will also sign you out.

Rewarded access offers. If you choose to unlock an ad-gated pass, you're taken to an offer wall operated by a third party (AdMaven). That provider sees its own technical data under its own policy. Zyrex only receives a completion signal so it can grant the pass — no profile data is exchanged in return.

05

Why we process your data

  • To deliver the service — signing you in, remembering preferences, serving files, syncing bookmarks and uploads.
  • To keep it fair — enforcing download limits, blocking scrapers, detecting ban evasion and stopping abuse.
  • To keep it secure — rate limiting, mitigating attacks, investigating malicious uploads and unauthorised access attempts.
  • To answer you — replying to support requests, appeals and copyright notices.
  • To improve the catalogue — aggregated, non-identifying counts of what gets downloaded, so the feed can surface what people actually want.

We don't use your data for automated decisions that produce legal effects, and we don't train advertising models on it.

06

Who we share with

Your data is not a product. It leaves our systems only in these situations:

Discord

Only during sign-in, through Discord's own OAuth2 flow. Their handling is governed by the Discord Privacy Policy.

Infrastructure providers

Hosting and CDN partners process requests on our behalf under contract. They don't use the data for their own marketing.

Offer wall provider

AdMaven, and only if you choose to complete a rewarded offer. You can ignore it and use the site without it.

Authorities and rights holders

Only where legally compelled, or where a valid copyright notice requires identifying an uploader.

If Zyrex is ever transferred to new ownership, account data would move with the service. You'd be told in advance, and this policy would continue to apply until replaced.

07

How long we keep things

Account data lives as long as your account does. Abuse-prevention logs roll off on a short cycle and are not archived. Access passes are erased once they expire. Support conversations are kept while the issue is open and for a short period afterwards, then deleted.

When you ask for deletion, profile data, preferences and upload metadata are removed. Three narrow exceptions remain: records we must keep to comply with a legal obligation, minimal information needed to keep a banned user from simply recreating the same account, and the security-incident records described in Section 09 while an intrusion investigation is open.

08

How we protect it

  • Sessions use signed, HTTP-only cookies with token verification on every protected request.
  • Transport is encrypted end to end, and administrative access is restricted and credential-protected.
  • Download links are short-lived and scoped, so a copied URL stops working quickly.
  • Uploads are scanned before publication, and anything suspicious is quarantined.

No system is perfectly safe. If a breach ever affects your data, we'll disclose it in the Discord server and by direct notice where we can, together with what was exposed and what we did about it.

Your part: enable two-factor authentication on Discord. Because sign-in depends on that account, its security is your account security.

09

Hacking, intrusion attempts and what we log

Attacking our servers is prohibited, and it is also one of the few situations where we keep records on purpose.

If someone tries to break into Zyrex — by exploiting a vulnerability, using stolen credentials, bypassing authentication or the download gate, flooding the service, or probing internal endpoints and storage — we collect and retain the technical evidence needed to stop it and to identify who did it.

What we recordWhyRetained

Hashed IP address

This evidence is used for security only: blocking the attack, patching the weakness, and — where the conduct is criminal or causes damage — reporting it to law enforcement or our legal advisors. We may share it with hosting and infrastructure providers strictly to the extent needed to stop an ongoing attack.

This data survives account deletion. If an account is removed while an intrusion investigation is open, the security records described above are kept until that investigation and any legal process conclude. This is the one exception to the deletion promises in Section 07, and it exists so that "delete my account" can't be used to erase evidence of an attack.

Responsible disclosure is welcome. If you find a vulnerability, email contact@zyrexediting.xyz with reproduction steps and give us a reasonable window to fix it. A good-faith report sent that way is not treated as an attack, and we won't keep security records against you for it.

10

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may hold some or all of the rights below. We honour them for everyone, regardless of jurisdiction.

Know what we hold

Ask for a copy of the personal data attached to your account.

Correct it

Most profile fields come from Discord — update them there and they follow you here.

Delete it

Request erasure of your account, uploads and associated records.

Object or restrict

Ask us to stop a particular use of your data and we'll assess it honestly.

Take it with you

Receive your account data in a readable, portable format.

Complain

Raise it with us first; you also retain the right to contact your local data authority.

Send any request to contact@zyrexediting.xyz from a channel we can verify. We respond within 30 days, usually much sooner, and we never charge for a first request.

11

Children

Zyrex isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, tell us and it will be removed promptly. Where local law sets a higher age of digital consent, that age applies instead.

12

International transfers

Our infrastructure and CDN providers operate globally, so your data may be processed in a country other than your own. Where that happens, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards — standard contractual clauses or an equivalent mechanism — and never widen what we're allowed to do with the data.

13

Changes to this policy

The version number and date at the top of this page always describe the text you're reading. If we change how data is collected or used in a meaningful way, we'll announce it in the Discord server before the change takes effect, and we'll keep the previous wording available so you can compare.
