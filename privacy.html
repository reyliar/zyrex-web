Privacy & Data

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Welcome to Zyrex. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring transparent handling of your data. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is used to deliver verified editing assets, and your rights as a creator in our community.

1. Information We Collect

We gather only the minimum data necessary to provide a fast, secure, and personalized creator experience:

  • Discord Profile Data: When logging in via Discord OAuth, we receive your public Discord ID, display name, username, and avatar hash.
  • Account Preferences: Your saved presets, liked assets, and interface settings stored with your profile.
  • Security & Integrity Logs: Timestamped download activity and IP hashing used strictly for rate limiting, DDoS protection, and alt account abuse prevention.
  • Voluntary Submissions: Information you provide when submitting presets, resources, support requests, or contacting our team.

2. How We Use Information

Your information is used strictly to power our services:

  • Authenticating your session and providing access to presets, plugins, sound packs, and scenepacks.
  • Syncing your personalized bookmarks, uploads, and account settings.
  • Generating secure, temporary, one-time download tokens to safeguard asset delivery.
  • Protecting our servers and community from automated spam, brute-force exploits, and abuse.
  • Analyzing anonymous aggregate download metrics to feature top-trending resources.

3. Data Storage & Protection

We treat creator security with highest priority. User sessions are secured using encrypted, signed HTTP-only cookies and robust token authentication. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data to any third party under any circumstance.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

Zyrex utilizes essential local storage and session cookies solely for functionality:

  • Session Authentication: Keeping you logged in securely as you browse between pages.
  • Local Preferences: Caching your view preference (grid/list mode), liked resources, and cookie consent choices.

We do not operate intrusive cross-site tracking cookies or third-party behavioral advertising scripts.

5. Third-Party Integrations

Authentication on Zyrex is facilitated through Discord OAuth2. When logging in, you interact directly with Discord's authorization endpoints. You may review their terms via Discord's Official Privacy Policy.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full control over your information:

  • You may log out at any time to instantly clear active session tokens from your browser.
  • You can request complete deletion of your associated data and upload records by contacting us via our Discord community.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or privacy inquiries regarding Zyrex, reach out directly through our official Discord Server or send an email to contact@zyrexediting.xyz.
